Observation Protocol

Protecting the Children's Sacred Flow State

In our environment, deep concentration is treated as sacred. To prevent disrupting the children's absorption during your tour, we ask all visitors to honor three simple observation agreements:

  • 1. Move Slowly and Softly: Walk around the perimeter of the room, being careful never to step on a child's floor work rug.
  • 2. Observe Without Interrupting: Please do not initiate conversation with children at work. If a child approaches you with a smile, respond warmly but briefly.
  • 3. Phones on Silent: Photography and recording are strictly prohibited during classroom hours to protect child privacy.

Visit Questions

Campus Visit Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful details to prepare for your observation morning.

Should we bring our child on the initial parent tour? +

We recommend that parents attend the initial 45-minute tour adults-only so you can focus completely on observing the work cycle and asking pedagogical questions. Once you decide to proceed with an application, your child is invited for their personal 30-minute classroom visit.

How far in advance should we book a tour? +

Because we limit tour groups to 4 families at a time to prevent disturbing the classrooms, tours typically book 2 to 3 weeks in advance. We encourage scheduling early.

Is parking available on campus? +

Yes, we provide dedicated visitor parking spaces directly in front of the main administrative atrium, including accessible stalls and EV charging stations.
