Transparent Policies

Frequently Asked Tuition Questions

Are all Montessori learning materials and meals included?

Yes. All certified AMI sensorial apparatus, math bead materials, phonetic readers, daily organic morning and afternoon snacks, and nature garden supplies are 100% included with no hidden supply fees.

Do you offer discounts for siblings?

Yes. Families with two or more concurrently enrolled children receive a 15% tuition reduction applied to the second and any subsequent sibling's enrollment.

What is your withdrawal or prorated refund policy?

If a family relocates or withdraws before a term begins, tuition is 100% refundable with 30 days written notice. Mid-term enrollments are automatically prorated for the exact number of instructional weeks remaining.

Equity & Inclusion

Our Commitment to Accessible Montessori Education

Dr. Maria Montessori founded her first Casa dei Bambini in the working-class San Lorenzo district of Rome. We honor that heritage by reserving 15% of our pod placements for families receiving need-based tuition assistance.

Our Financial Aid Committee uses an anonymous third-party evaluation to award sliding-scale tuition reductions ranging from 20% to 60% of total tuition. Financial assistance is awarded without bias toward race, creed, or family structure.

Financial Transparency

Tuition Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding installment options, deposits, and payment methods.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We accept automated ACH bank transfers, debit cards, and major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex) through the encrypted Parent Portal. Automatic monthly billing occurs on the 1st of each calendar month.

Is there an enrollment deposit required upon acceptance? +

Yes, upon formal acceptance of enrollment, a one-time non-refundable deposit of $250 is required to secure your child's placement in the micro-pod. This deposit is fully credited toward your child's first month of tuition.

Are there tuition discounts for annual upfront payments? +

Yes! Families who elect to pay the entire academic year (10 months) upfront receive a 10% discount off total tuition fees.
